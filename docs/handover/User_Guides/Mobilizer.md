# Mobilizer guide

You lead one ward (or one registration centre in an MCA race). You register supporters,
schedule and count your events, and see your own payments. You see only your own ward.

Most of this is done on a phone.

## Getting a login

Either the campaign adds you, or you sign up yourself:

1. Open the team sign-up link or scan the QR code the campaign shared. The page is **Join the
   campaign team**.
2. Under **I want to be**, pick **Mobilizer**.
3. Fill **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **ID number** and **Ward you will
   work**. In an MCA race you pick **Ward of your registration centre** and then the
   **Registration centre**. Tick the consent box.
4. Press **Sign up**. A coordinator or the manager reviews it.
5. Once approved you get an email with a link. Open it, fill **New password** and **The same
   password again** (at least 8 characters), and press **Save and sign in**. The link works
   for 72 hours.

Your ID number is seen only by you, the candidate and the campaign manager.

## Signing in

1. Open the sign-in page (`/signin`).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. If the installation has Google set up, you can press **Sign in with Google** instead.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. The link works for an hour.
3. Choose a new password and press **Save and sign in**.

### Signing out

Press your name at the top right, then **Sign out**. Signing out clears any entry kept on the
phone that was not sent yet, so send your entries first.

## What you see

On a phone, a tab bar sits at the bottom of the screen with your four pages, one tap away:

- **My events**
- **Register supporter**
- **My supporters**
- **My payments**

On a computer the same pages are in the menu on the left, under **My work**. **Your access**
shows **Mobilizer** and "Your own ward."

## How to

### Register a supporter

1. Tap **Register supporter**. The page is **Register a supporter**.
2. Fill **Full name** and **Phone**, and pick the **Ward** (and **Registration centre** in an
   MCA race; your own centre is picked to start).
3. Under **Support**, tap **Supporter**, **Undecided** or **Opposed**.
4. Read the consent wording to the person. Tick the box only if they agree.
5. Tap **Register supporter**. You see **Registered.** Tap **Register another** for the next.

What you type is kept on the phone until it is sent. If you lose signal you see **Not sent: no
network.** Your entry is kept. Tap **Try again** when you have signal.

### Count an event

1. Tap **My events**. The next event is at the top.
2. Use the **+** and **−** buttons (or type the number) for **Reached** and **Attended**.
3. Tap **Save attendance**. Saving marks the event done.
4. To count a different event, tap **Count** beside it under **Coming up**.

Counts are kept on this phone until they are saved, even if the page reloads. With no
network you see **Not sent: no network.** and a **Try again** button.

### Schedule an event

1. On **My events** tap **Schedule event**.
2. Fill **Event title**, **Ward** (and **Registration centre** in an MCA race), **Venue** and
   **Date**.
3. Tap **Schedule event**.

### Show the sign-up QR at an event

1. On **My events** find the event: the next one is at the top, the rest are under **Coming
   up**, and finished ones are under **Past**.
2. Tap **Sign-up QR**. People scan it to sign up as supporters. If the button reads **Closed**,
   sign-ups for that event have been closed.
3. **Copy link** or **Share on WhatsApp** sends the link instead. **Print** prints the code.

### See your supporters

Tap **My supporters**. The tiles count **On the register**, **Supporters**, **Undecided** and
**Consent missing**. Use the **Support** filter to narrow the list. In an MCA race, a
supporter marked **Needs a centre** can be given one from the list on that row.

### See your payments

Tap **My payments**. Each row shows the date, what it was for, the amount, its status
(**Scheduled**, **Sending**, **Paid**, **To retry**, **Cancelled**) and the reference. You are
paid only for work the campaign assigned to you and marked done.

## Who to ask for help

Press **Help** at the top: it lists the campaign's phone and email. Your coordinator or the
campaign manager decides your ward and your paid work.

## Notes to confirm

- **Invite** appears only on **Past** event cards, and lets a mobilizer **Preview** an
  invitation but not send it; only the candidate or the manager sends texts.
