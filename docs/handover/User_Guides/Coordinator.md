# Coordinator guide

You run part of the campaign: the wards (or registration centres) the manager gave you as
your area. You see and change only what is inside your area. You approve mobilizers and
agents who sign up there, plan events, give paid work and schedule its payment. You do not
approve payments or send texts yourself.

## Signing in

1. Open the sign-in page (`/signin`).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. If the installation has Google set up, you can press **Sign in with Google** instead.

Your first sign-in comes from an email link. In practice mode no email is sent, so ask whoever added you for the link. Fill **New password** and **The same password
again** (at least 8 characters) and press **Save and sign in**. That link works for 72 hours.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. The link works for an hour.
3. Choose a new password and press **Save and sign in**.
4. If you see **This link no longer works**, press **Send a new link**.

### Signing out

Press your name at the top right, then **Sign out**.

## What you see

- **Plan:** Overview, Strategy, Wards
- **Field:** Events, Mobilizers, Agents, Assignments, Supporters, Volunteers
- **Reach out:** Messages
- **People:** Sign-ups
- **Money:** Payments
- **Recruit:** Recruitment, only in the party's own workspace

**Your access** at the bottom of the menu shows **Coordinator** and names your wards
("Limited to ..."). If you see no wards at all, your area is not set yet: ask the manager.

Use the **Area** picker in the top bar to look at one of your wards at a time.

## How to

### Approve people who signed up in your area

1. Open **Sign-ups**. Share the **Team sign-up link** with **Copy link**, or show its QR code.
2. Under **Waiting**, press **Approve** for each person you accept. They get an email link to
   choose a password.
3. To turn someone down, press **Reject**, type the reason, then **Reject with this reason**.

You do not see ID numbers. Only the candidate and the manager do.

### Add or move a mobilizer

1. Open **Mobilizers** and press **Add mobilizer**.
2. Fill **Full name** and **Phone** and pick the **Ward** (or **Registration centre**).
3. To let them sign in, tick **Give them a login, so they can record their own events and
   supporters.** and fill **Username** and **Email**.
4. Press **Save mobilizer**. Use **Edit** on a row to change a name, phone or ward.

### Ready agents

1. Open **Agents**. **Centres with no agent** shows the gaps in your area.
2. On each row set the status (Applied, Vetted, Trained, Ready) and the **Training date**.
   They save as soon as you change them.

### Plan and record events

1. Open **Events** and press **Schedule event**.
2. Fill **Event title**, **Ward** (and **Registration centre** in an MCA race), **Venue** and
   **Date**, then press **Schedule event**.
3. After the event press **Record**. Fill **How many were reached / invited** and **How many
   attended**, then **Save & mark done**.
4. **Sign-up QR** shows a code people scan to sign up as supporters. **Print** prints it.
5. **Invite** lets you write an invitation and press **Preview** to see how many it reaches.
   Only the candidate or the manager can send it.

The bell at the top tells you when a past event still needs its attendance recorded.

### Give paid work and schedule its payment

1. Press **Assign work** on **Assignments**, on a mobilizer or agent row, or **Assign** on an
   event card.
2. Pick the **Kind**, **Who**, the **Ward** or **What**, and the **Amount (KES)**. Press
   **Assign**.
3. When the work is done, open **Assignments**, view **To mark done**, and press **Mark done**
   (or **Cancel** if it did not happen).
4. Open **Payments**, press **Schedule payment**, pick the **Work done**, and press
   **Schedule**. It waits for the campaign manager to approve.

### Draft a text to supporters

1. Open **Messages**. Under **New SMS**, pick your wards in **Send to** and write the
   **Message**. The number of recipients shows below.
2. Press **Send for approval**. The candidate or manager sends it. You can **Delete** your own
   draft while it waits.

What you type stays on this device until it is sent.

### Volunteers

Open **Volunteers**. Search by name, phone or ward. Press **Make active** after the first
call, or **Make inactive**.

### Recruit party members (party workspace only)

1. Open **Recruitment**. **Your link** shows your link to the **Join the Party** form and its
   QR code.
2. Press **Copy link** to share it. **Replace link** stops the old link and QR code.
3. **Applicants through your link** counts applicants by status. Membership Officers still
   decide each application.

## Who to ask for help

Press **Help** at the top: it lists the campaign's phone and email. Your manager sets your
area. For a password reset for someone else, ask the superuser.

## Notes to confirm

- **Supporters** shows a **Sign-up link** button, but the server lets only the candidate and
  the manager open the campaign-wide sign-up link. A coordinator who presses it gets a refusal.
  Use an event's **Sign-up QR** instead.
- The design gives coordinators in the party workspace a read-only list of party members in
  their area. No menu item for it was found.
