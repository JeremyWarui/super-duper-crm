# Nominations Officer guide

You run the party's nominations. You draft the nominations timetable, vet members'
applications, move each nomination through its stages, mark fees paid, and create the
campaign workspace for an approved aspirant. You work in the Party CRM with a party staff
login.

## Signing in

1. Open the sign-in page (`/signin`).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. If the installation has Google set up, you can press **Sign in with Google** instead.

Your first sign-in comes from an email link. Fill **New password** and **The same password
again** (at least 8 characters) and press **Save and sign in**. That link works for 72 hours.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. The link works for an hour.
3. Choose a new password and press **Save and sign in**.

### Signing out

Press your name at the top right, then **Sign out**.

## What you see

The Party CRM menu shows **Aspirants**. Under your name it shows **Nominations Officer**.

## How to

### Draft the nominations timetable

Members can apply only inside a published timetable.

1. On **Aspirants**, in **Nominations timetable**, press **Draft a timetable**.
2. Fill **Title** (for example "Nominations 2027"), **Opens** and **Closes**.
3. Under **Seats and nomination fees (KES)** tick each seat that is open (MCA, MP, Governor,
   Senator, Women Rep) and type its fee. Leave 0 for no fee.
4. Write the **Requirements** aspirants must meet.
5. Press **Save draft**. Press **Edit** to change a draft.

The Party Super Admin publishes it. You cannot publish your own draft: it shows "Someone else
publishes it."

### Vet an application

1. Filter the list with **Status** and **Seat filter**. Press a name to open the nomination.
2. You see when it was nominated and decided, the timetable it was made in, whether the
   declaration is signed, the fee, and the attached documents. Press a document to open it.
3. Type a **Reason** and press the next step. The buttons depend on the stage: **Mark
   submitted** (for a draft), **Approve**, **Reject**, **Withdraw**, **Suspend** or
   **Reinstate**. Rejecting and suspending need a reason.

### Mark a nomination fee paid

The system takes no payments. When the aspirant has paid as the party's instructions say:

1. Open the nomination while it is still a draft.
2. Type the **M-Pesa or bank reference** in **Fee reference**.
3. Press **Mark fee paid**. The aspirant can then submit.

### Nominate a member yourself

1. Press **Nominate a member**.
2. In **Find a member** type a name, or their whole phone number. Only approved members
   without a live nomination are offered. Press the right person.
3. Pick the **Seat**, then the **County**, **Constituency** and **Ward** down to the level the
   seat is fought over.
4. Press **Nominate**.

### Create the campaign workspace for an approved aspirant

1. Open an **Approved** nomination that has no workspace yet. **Create the campaign
   workspace** shows at the bottom.
2. Fill **Campaign title** (optional), **Election date** (optional), **Aspirant's username**
   and **Aspirant's email**.
3. To add a manager now, fill **Manager's username** and **Manager's email**. You can leave
   them empty; the aspirant can add a manager later.
4. Press **Create workspace**. A notice confirms each login and whether its link was emailed.
   Press **Done** after each.

The workspace is open while the aspirant stays approved. If you suspend or withdraw the
aspirant, their campaign team sees that the campaign is closed; its data is kept.

## Who to ask for help

Press **Help** at the top. It lists the party office's phone and email. The Party Super Admin
can reset a password, change your office, or explain a page.
