# Agent guide

You are a polling agent. The campaign recruits, vets and trains you during the campaign and
gives you one registration centre, so you are ready for polling day. This system prepares
agents; it does nothing on polling day itself.

Most of this is done on a phone.

## Getting a login

1. Open the team sign-up link or scan the QR code the campaign shared. The page is **Join the
   campaign team**.
2. Under **I want to be**, pick **Agent**.
3. Fill **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone**, **Email** and **ID number**. Pick the **Ward
   of your registration centre**, then the **Registration centre**. Tick the consent box.
4. Press **Sign up**. A coordinator or the manager reviews it.
5. Once approved you get an email with a link. Open it, fill **New password** and **The same
   password again** (at least 8 characters), and press **Save and sign in**. The link works
   for 72 hours.

Your ID number is seen only by you, the candidate and the campaign manager.

## Signing in

1. Open the sign-in page (`/signin`).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. If the installation has Google set up, you can press **Sign in with Google** instead.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. The link works for an hour.
3. Choose a new password and press **Save and sign in**.

### Signing out

Press your name at the top right, then **Sign out**.

## What you see

On a phone, a tab bar sits at the bottom of the screen with your three pages:

- **My centre**
- **My events**
- **My payments**

On a computer the same pages are in the menu on the left, under **My work**. **Your access**
shows **Agent** and "Your centre and assignments."

## How to

### Check your centre and progress

1. Tap **My centre**.
2. **Registration centre** names your centre and its ward.
3. **Before polling day** shows the four steps, **Applied**, **Vetted**, **Trained** and
   **Ready**, each marked **Done** or **Not yet**. The campaign moves you through them.
4. The training date shows under the steps once the campaign sets it.

### See your assignments

On **My centre**, **Your assignments** lists the work the campaign gave you, with the amount
and its state: **To do**, **Done** or **Cancelled**.

### See events

Tap **My events**. Pick **Upcoming** or **Past**. You can read the events; you do not record
them.

### See your payments

Tap **My payments** (or the **My payments** link under **Your details** on **My centre**).
Each row shows the date, what it was for, the amount, the status (**Scheduled**, **Sending**,
**Paid**, **To retry**, **Cancelled**) and the reference. You are paid only for an assignment
the campaign marked done.

### Check your details

**Your details** on **My centre** shows your name, phone and ID number. To correct them, ask
your coordinator or the campaign manager.

## Who to ask for help

Press **Help** at the top: it lists the campaign's phone and email. Your coordinator or the
campaign manager sets your centre, your training date and your paid work.

Agent pages are for reading. There is no form to fill, so nothing waits on the phone to be
sent.
