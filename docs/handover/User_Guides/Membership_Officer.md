# Membership Officer guide

You keep the party's member and volunteer registers. You decide membership applications,
look after volunteers, log requests about personal data, and keep the county branch
officials up to date. You work in the Party CRM with a party staff login.

## Signing in

1. Open the sign-in page (`/signin`).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. If the installation has Google set up, you can press **Sign in with Google** instead.

Your first sign-in comes from an email link. Fill **New password** and **The same password
again** (at least 8 characters) and press **Save and sign in**. That link works for 72 hours.

If your staff login is linked to your own party membership, you first see **Where to?**: pick
**Party CRM** for this work, or **My membership** for your own member card.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. The link works for an hour.
3. Choose a new password and press **Save and sign in**.

### Signing out

Press your name at the top right, then **Sign out**.

## What you see

The Party CRM menu shows:

- **Members**
- **Volunteers**
- **Data requests**
- **Branches**

Under your name it shows **Membership Officer**. The top bar has **Search** (or Ctrl K) for
finding a member by name or phone, the notifications bell, and **Help**.

## How to

### Decide a membership application

1. Open **Members**. Set **Status** to **Pending** to see who is waiting.
2. Narrow the list with **Search members** (name, phone or email), **County**,
   **Constituency** and **Ward**.
3. Press a name to open the member. You see their phone, email, when they applied, and the
   consent they gave.
4. Type a **Reason** if you want one recorded, then press **Approve**, **Reject** or
   **Withdraw**.
5. For an approved member the buttons are **Suspend** and **Withdraw**. For a suspended
   member they are **Reinstate** and **Withdraw**.

A member who applied through a coordinator's recruitment link still needs your decision.

### Erase a member's details on request

1. Open the member. Erasing works only for a **Withdrawn** or **Rejected** member.
2. Press **Erase their details**, then **Yes, erase**. Their name, phone, email and account
   are erased for good.

### Export the register

On **Members** (or **Volunteers**) set the filters you want and press **Export CSV**.

### Look after volunteers

1. Open **Volunteers**. Filter by **Search volunteers**, **Status**, **Area** (how they want
   to help) and place.
2. Press a name to open the volunteer.
3. Write **Availability notes** and press **Save notes**.
4. Press **Activate** once you have spoken to them (only for an approved member), or
   **Deactivate**.
5. For a withdrawn volunteer, **Erase volunteer details** then **Yes, erase** removes their
   volunteer details. Their membership stays.

### Log a data request

1. Open **Data requests** and press **Log a request**.
2. Pick the **Request** (**Access to their data**, **Correction** or **Erasure**) and the
   **Received on** date. The due date follows: 7 days for access, 14 for correction or
   erasure.
3. Fill **Who asked**, **Phone or email to answer** and **What they asked for**, then press
   **Log request**.
4. When you have dealt with it, press **Answer**, write **How it was answered**, and press
   **Mark answered**. **Save** keeps a note without closing it. **Reopen** opens it again.

Overdue requests show an **Overdue** badge.

### Keep branch officials up to date

1. Open **Branches** and press a county name.
2. **Officials** shows each office: **Chairperson**, **Secretary**, **Treasurer**,
   **Organising secretary**, **Youth leader**, **Women leader**, with **In office**,
   **Vacant** or **Membership lapsed**.
3. To appoint, under **Appoint** pick the **Office**, the **Reason** (**Elected at a branch
   meeting** or **Interim appointment**) and **Effective from**.
4. In **Member** type two or more letters of the name or phone, then pick the person. Only
   approved members of that county are offered.
5. Attach the **Signed minutes of the meeting** (a PDF or a photo) and add a **Note** if
   needed. Press **Appoint**. The current holder's term ends the day before.
6. To end a term, press **End term**, set **Last day** and **Why**, then **End the term**.
7. **Past officials** lists earlier holders.

The branch office address, next meeting and whether the branch shows on the website are set
by the Party Super Admin.

## Who to ask for help

Press **Help** at the top. It lists the party office's phone and email. The Party Super Admin
can reset a password, change your office, or explain a page.
