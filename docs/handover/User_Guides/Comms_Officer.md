# Comms Officer guide (party office)

You write the party's text messages to members and volunteers. The Party Super Admin
approves and sends each one; nothing you draft goes out until then. You work in the Party
CRM with a party staff login.

This guide is for the party office. A campaign's comms officer has its own guide:
[Comms.md](Comms.md).

## Signing in

1. Open the sign-in page (`/signin`).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. If the installation has Google set up, you can press **Sign in with Google** instead.

Your first sign-in comes from an email link. Fill **New password** and **The same password
again** (at least 8 characters) and press **Save and sign in**. That link works for 72 hours.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. The link works for an hour.
3. Choose a new password and press **Save and sign in**.

### Signing out

Press your name at the top right, then **Sign out**.

## What you see

The Party CRM menu shows **Messages**. Under your name it shows **Comms Officer**.

## How to

### Draft a message

1. Open **Messages**. Under **New message**, pick the **Audience**:
   - **Members**: members who ticked SMS updates when they joined.
   - **Volunteers**: active volunteers who chose SMS as their contact.
2. To reach one place only, pick a **County**, then a **Constituency** and **Ward** if you
   want to narrow it further. Leave them on **All counties** for everyone.
3. The line below counts the recipients. People who replied STOP are left out.
4. Write the **Message**. Below it you see the characters, the SMS parts, and that "Reply
   STOP to opt out" is added.
5. Press **Send for approval**. You see "Saved. The Party Super Admin approves it before it is
   sent."

### Follow your drafts

- **Waiting for approval** lists drafts not yet decided, with who drafted them, the audience,
  the place and how many recipients they had when drafted.
- **Recent sends** lists what went out, with the delivery counts (**Delivered**, **Waiting**,
  **Failed**, **Practice**), or **Declined** with the reason.

**Practice** means the installation has no SMS provider switched on yet: the text is logged,
not sent.

## Who to ask for help

Press **Help** at the top. It lists the party office's phone and email. The Party Super Admin
approves your messages, and can reset a password or change your office.
