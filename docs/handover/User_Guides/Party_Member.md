# Party member guide

As a party member you have your own account, **My membership**, at `/account`. It holds your
member card, your volunteering, party events, your application to vie for a seat, and your
data and privacy choices.

This applies to the party's installation. An aspirant's own campaign site has no member
accounts.

## Becoming a member

1. Open the join page (`/join-party`). The sign-in page links to it: **Not a member yet? Join
   the party**.
2. Fill **First name**, **Surname**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Password** (at least 8
   characters). Press **Continue**.
3. Pick your **County**, **Constituency**, **Ward** and **Polling station**. To volunteer
   too, tick **Volunteer for events** and answer **Profession**, **How would you like to
   help?** and **Preferred contact**. Press **Continue**.
4. Under **Consent**, tick the consent box. The SMS updates box is optional. Press **Join the
   party**.
5. A Membership Officer reviews your application. Once the party approves you, sign in with
   that email and password.

## Signing in

1. Open the sign-in page (`/signin`).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. If the installation has Google set up, you can press **Sign in with Google** instead.

You land on your account. The page greets you with **Habari** and your first name.

If you also work for the party or a campaign with another login linked to your membership,
signing in with that login shows **Where to?**. Pick **My membership** for your own account.

### Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. The link works for an hour.
3. Choose a new password (**New password**, **The same password again**) and press **Save and
   sign in**.
4. If you see **This link no longer works**, press **Send a new link**.

### Signing out

Press **Sign out** in the site header.

## What you see

At the top, your **Member card** (branch, polling station, member since) and **Membership
status**. Below it, four tabs:

- **Volunteering**
- **Party events**
- **My nomination**
- **My data & privacy**

## How to

### Volunteer for the party

1. Open **Volunteering**.
2. Pick your **Profession**. Under **How would you like to help?** tick one or more ways.
3. Pick your **Preferred contact** (Call, SMS, WhatsApp or Email) and tick the consent box.
4. Press **Volunteer for events**.
5. Later, press **Change my volunteer details**, edit, and press **Save my volunteer
   details**.

To volunteer for a candidate's campaign instead, use the candidate's profile page on the
party website.

### See party events

Open **Party events**. Press an event's title to read it.

### Apply to vie for a seat

You can apply only while a nominations timetable is open. Otherwise the tab says when
applications open.

1. Open **My nomination**. Under **Which seat are you vying for?** pick the seat. You vie in
   your own registered area.
2. Press **Start application**.
3. Add each document: type its name in **Document** (for example "National ID"), choose the
   **File (PDF or photo)**, and press **Add document**. **Remove** takes one off.
4. If there is a **Nomination fee**, pay it as the party's instructions say. The nominations
   office marks it paid; you cannot submit until then.
5. Tick the declaration and press **Submit application**.
6. **Withdraw** (or **Withdraw application** after submitting) takes your application back.

### Your data and privacy

1. Open **My data & privacy**. **My details** shows what the party holds. To correct your
   name, phone, ward or polling station, contact the party office (its contacts show there).
2. **My consents** shows what you agreed to and when.
3. **Stop volunteering** ends your volunteering and keeps your membership.
4. **Leave the party**, then **Yes, leave the party**, ends your membership and signs you out.
   **Keep my membership** goes back.
5. **Read the privacy notice** opens the party's notice.

## Who to ask for help

Contact the party office. Its phone and email show on **My data & privacy** and on the party
website.
